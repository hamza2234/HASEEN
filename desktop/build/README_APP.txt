Hybrid PSO-GA Feature Selection System
======================================

Master's thesis of Hussein Naser Hussein
Damghan University - Computer Science (Soft Computing and Artificial Intelligence)
Supervisor: Dr. Abdolali Basiri
May 2026

HOW TO RUN
----------
Double-click HybridPSOGA.exe (or the desktop shortcut).
Choose Arabic or English from the bottom of the sidebar.
Everything works offline; no internet connection is required.

WHAT IT DOES
------------
* Runs the full thesis pipeline: normalization, train/test split, PSO-GA
  feature selection, Random Forest training and evaluation.
* Lets you paste an email or type a website URL and get a verdict
  (fake / real) with the confidence and the attributes behind it.
* Shows every number published in the thesis next to the value this
  application computes, plus the contradictions found inside the thesis.

UNINSTALL
---------
Settings > Apps > Installed apps > "Hybrid PSO-GA Feature Selection System"
> Uninstall.  No internet connection is needed at any point.
